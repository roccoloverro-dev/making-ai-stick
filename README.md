# Making AI Stick Simulation

A round-by-round change management simulation from the Making AI Stick course.

You're the change lead for an AI drafting assistant at a fictional federal benefits agency. You have 100 change credits and four phases to get 4,200 caseworkers using it in their real work. At month 18 you find out whether it stuck. The simulation is scored on the Six Conditions of Durable Change.

**Play it:** https://roccoloverro-dev.github.io/making-ai-stick/

- It takes about 40 minutes, plus the debrief.
- It works on laptops, tablets, and phones.
- It works solo or in pods of 4 to 5 sharing one screen.
- Nothing you enter leaves your browser. Your progress and results are saved only on your device.
